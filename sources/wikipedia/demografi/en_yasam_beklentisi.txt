Turkey is [[Administrative divisions of Turkey|administratively divided]] into 81 provinces.

According to estimation of the [[Turkish Statistical Institute]] (TurkStat), [[life expectancy]] at birth in [[Turkey]] in period 2021–2023 was '''77.3''' years ('''74.7''' years for male and '''80.0''' years for female).<ref name="TurkStat_LE">{{cite web|url=https://data.tuik.gov.tr/Kategori/GetKategori?p=nufus-ve-demografi-109&dil=2 |title=Data Portal for Statistics: Population and Demography |lang=en |work=TurkStat |date=18 September 2024 |access-date=12 May 2025 |quote=statistical table 'Life expectancy at birth by provinces and sex'}}</ref><br>
[[Healthy life expectancy]] for this period was '''57.6''' years ('''59.0''' years for male and '''56.3''' years for female).<ref name="TurkStat_HLE">{{cite web|url=https://data.tuik.gov.tr/Kategori/GetKategori?p=nufus-ve-demografi-109&dil=2 |title=Data Portal for Statistics: Population and Demography |lang=en |work=TurkStat |date=18 September 2024 |access-date=12 May 2025 |quote=statistical table 'Healthy Life Years'}}</ref>

According to alternative estimation of the [[United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs|United Nations]], in 2023 life expectancy in Turkey was '''77.2''' years total ('''74.5''' for male, '''79.9''' for female).<ref name="un_prospects">{{cite web|url=https://population.un.org/wpp/downloads?folder=Standard%20Projections&group=Most%20used |title=World Population Prospects 2024 |publisher=[[United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs]] |format=XLSX |lang=en |date=27 July 2024}} — see file "Compact (most used: estimates and medium projections)"</ref>

Estimation of the [[World Bank Group]] is similar: 77.2 years (74.5 for male, 79.9 for female).<ref>{{cite web|title=Life expectancy at birth, total|url=https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.IN?locations=TR|publisher=The World Bank Group|date=15 April 2025|access-date=28 April 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Life expectancy at birth, male|url=https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.MA.IN?locations=TR|publisher=The World Bank Group|date=15 April 2025|access-date=28 April 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Life expectancy at birth, female|url=https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.FE.IN?locations=TR|publisher=The World Bank Group|date=15 April 2025|access-date=28 April 2025}}</ref>

Estimation of [[Eurostat]] for 2023: '''77.3''' years total ('''74.8''' for male, '''79.8''' for female).<ref>{{cite web|title=Life expectancy at birth by sex |url=https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-datasets/-/tps00205 |publisher=[[Eurostat]] |language=en |date=5 June 2025 |access-date=24 June 2025}} — Dataset</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Life expectancy at birth by sex |url=https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/tps00205/default/table |publisher=[[Eurostat]] |language=en |date=5 June 2025 |access-date=24 June 2025}} — Table. For visualization use the tab "[https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/tps00205/default/map Map]"</ref>

According to estimation of the [[World Health Organization|WHO]] for 2019, at that year life expectancy in Turkey was 77.62 years (75.09 years for male and 80.13 years for female).<br>
And healthy life expectancy was 67.24 years (66.63 years for male and 67.80 years for female).<ref name="who">{{cite web |title=The Global Health Observatory: Life expectancy and Healthy life expectancy |url=https://www.who.int/data/gho/data/themes/topics/indicator-groups/indicator-group-details/GHO/life-expectancy-and-healthy-life-expectancy |language=en |date=2 August 2024 |access-date=4 December 2025 |publisher=[[World Health Organization]]}}</ref>

In 2015–17, the difference in life expectancy between the most prosperous and disadvantaged provinces was 4.55 years.<br>
In 2018–20, this difference was 4.69 years.<br>
In 2021–23, this difference was 4.71 years.<ref name="TurkStat_LE" />
